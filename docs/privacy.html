Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 2026

Introduction

My Bookshelf is a personal book collection manager designed to help you organize your reading life. We are committed to protecting your privacy and being transparent about how we handle your data.

This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and your rights regarding your personal data.

Information We Collect

Data Type Purpose Storage
Email address Account identification (Google Sign-In) Firebase Authentication
Bookshelves & books Core app functionality Local device; Firebase Firestore (if signed in)
Book Club data Collaborative shelves, reviews, comments Firebase Firestore (requires sign-in)
Shelf preferences Shelf styles, ordering, display settings Local device only

What We Don't Collect

  • We do not collect analytics or usage data
  • We do not use advertising trackers
  • We do not access your contacts, location, camera, or other device data
  • We do not track what books you search for or read

How We Use Your Information

Your data is used solely to provide the app's functionality:

  • Email address: To identify your account and enable cloud sync
  • Bookshelves & books: To display and manage your personal collection
  • Book Club data: To enable shared shelves, reviews, and comments with other members
  • Shelf preferences: To remember your display customizations

We do not sell, share, or use your data for advertising purposes.

Data Storage and Security

Local Storage (Default)

All your bookshelf data is stored locally on your device using a Room database. You can use the app entirely offline as a guest without creating an account. Your data never leaves your device unless you sign in.

Cloud Sync (When Signed In)

When you sign in with Google, your bookshelves and books are synced to Firebase Firestore. Book Club features require sign-in and store shared data in Firestore.

  • Data is encrypted in transit using HTTPS/TLS
  • Data is stored in Google Cloud infrastructure with industry-standard security
  • Only you can access your personal data through your authenticated account
  • Book Club data is accessible only to club members

Third-Party Services

Firebase Authentication (Google)

Used for secure sign-in with your Google account. Firebase Authentication stores your email address and authentication tokens. Firebase Privacy Policy

Firebase Firestore (Google)

Used for cloud sync of your bookshelf data and Book Club features. Google Cloud Privacy

Open Library API (Internet Archive)

Used to search for books and retrieve book metadata (titles, authors, covers). Search queries are sent to Open Library's public API. No personal data is shared. Open Library Privacy Policy

Your Rights

You have full control over your data:

Access Your Data

All your bookshelves, books, and Book Club activity are visible within the app at any time.

Delete Your Data

You can request deletion of your account and all associated cloud data. See our account deletion page for instructions. Upon processing your request, the following is permanently removed:

  • All bookshelves and books from cloud storage
  • All Book Club memberships, reviews, and comments
  • Your Firebase Authentication account

Local data on your device is not affected by account deletion. To remove local data, sign out within the app or uninstall it.

Use Offline

You can use the app entirely offline as a guest. Your data stays on your device only. Book Clubs require sign-in as they are shared features.

Contact Information

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or your data, please contact us by opening an issue on our GitHub repository.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date. Continued use of the app after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
