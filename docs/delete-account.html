Delete Your Account

How to Delete Your Account

You can delete your account and all associated data directly within the My Bookshelf app.

1
Open the app Launch My Bookshelf on your device
2
Open the menu Tap the three-dot menu icon in the top bar
3
Sign Out Select "Sign Out" to disconnect your Google account
4
Confirm sign out Review the confirmation and confirm to sign out and clear your cloud data
Warning:

Signing out removes all synced data from the cloud and revokes Book Club memberships. Local bookshelf data remains on your device. To fully remove local data, uninstall the app.

What Gets Deleted

When you sign out and delete your account data, the following is permanently removed from the cloud:

  • All bookshelves and books synced to the cloud
  • All Book Club memberships, reviews, and comments
  • Your Firebase Authentication session

Local data on your device (bookshelves, books) is retained so you can continue using the app as a guest. To remove local data, uninstall the app.

Guest Users

If you use My Bookshelf without signing in, no data is stored in the cloud. All your data is local to your device. Simply uninstall the app to remove all data.
